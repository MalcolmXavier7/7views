# Side View .1107: the community server

`server.json` describes the Side View community Discord: its roles, channels, permissions, forum tags, AutoMod rule and the welcome, rules, roadmap, legal-sources and how-to-cast posts.

## What's in it

- **Private, invite-only.** Only Founder and Mods can make invites. It's never listed in Server Discovery.
- **Roles:** Founder, Mod, Beta Tester, Member.
- **START HERE:** #welcome, #rules, #announcements, #roadmap (read-only).
- **SIDE VIEW:**
  - #setups: a forum with one post per setup, including its layout code.
  - #legal-sources and #tips-and-shortcuts.
  - #feature-requests and #bug-reports: forums with tags.
  - #beta-builds: Beta Testers only.
- **WATCH TOGETHER:**
  - #game-night-chat.
  - **Watch Party** voice: screen sharing is off, so nobody rebroadcasts a game.
  - **Side View Live** stage: mods bring people up to cast their setup.
- **COMMUNITY:** #general, #clips, #off-topic. **STAFF:** #mod-room, #discord-updates.
- **AutoMod** blocks links to well-known unlicensed stream sites.

## The line we hold

Side View is a viewer. The server is for the tool and its culture: setups, tips, ideas and watching together. Two rules keep it legal:

1. **Legal sources only.** Official, licensed or free-and-legal streams. No links to unlicensed stream sites.
2. **Never rebroadcast what isn't yours.** Cast your setup, the app or your own content. At watch parties, everyone watches on their own screen.
