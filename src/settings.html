      Cast your setup

      1. Load only what you're allowed to show: your own content, an official free stream that allows it, or empty screens.
      2. In Discord, join the 7 Views Live stage and request to speak.
      3. Click Share Your Screen and pick the 7 Views window.

      Never cast a game, show or movie you don't have the rights to. Watch parties: everyone watches on their own screen.

      General

      Log in once and every screen is signed in. Changing this reloads the screens.

      Logins from Chrome

      1. (or type chrome://password-manager/settings in Chrome).
      2. Under "Export passwords", click Download file and confirm with your Windows PIN or password.
      3. Come back here, click Import, and pick the file Chrome saved.

      That file holds every password in plain text.

      Saved logins are encrypted with your Windows account and only filled into pages from the matching site.
